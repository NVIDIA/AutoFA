# Steer and contribute deliberately

AutoFA normally investigates and implements improvements. Direct inspection, experiments,
repairs and authored/imported candidates are also supported when useful and within scope;
no requirement to exhaust all native alternatives. Protect requested unassisted arms and
avoid duplicating healthy work.

Treat assistance as an agreed capability, not a mandatory escalation ladder. The operational
playbooks include: source/trace/image inspection; narrow-to-broad causal candidate synthesis;
authored unscored detectors; guarded repairs; classifier comparison; cross-run unscored transfer
and source-qualified leads; live goal/worker tuning; bounded recovery; diagnosed runtime fixes;
and finalization/publication recovery. Choose from current evidence, not historical detector
names. Record apply/hold/reject decisions and failed attempts as carefully as successful changes.

## Diagnose direction

Separate actual misses from unscored, sampled, stale, abstained, failed or disputed evidence.
Check fixed denominators, per-detector recall, deduplicated union, overlap and per-source
breakdown. Low early estimates can reflect unfinished work. A changed denominator or
provisional-to-final relabeling is not improvement.

Ask AutoFA to investigate uncovered sources/tasks, compare broad causal families and narrow
members, or repair a contradiction. Prefer native family discovery, independent parent
execution, hard negatives, synthesis, classifier profiles and guarded repair. Allow a bounded
meaningful phase, inspect its response, and record guidance delivered/consumed/addressed before
repeating it. More rounds alone do not resolve a repeated blocker.

Use a discovery-only steering channel with known audience and safe-boundary behavior. Never
put operational guidance into shared analysis-skill snapshots that also reach judges. Preserve
judge rubric, thresholds, review strictness and admission policy. If the driver accidentally
changed judge context, restore it under native compatibility/exposure rules, record affected
evidence, and do not conceal the mistake or force incompatible grades current.

## Experiments and configuration

Record hypothesis, baseline, fixed development inputs, retained positive/negative controls,
cost and apply/hold/reject/revert decision. Use native classifier comparison/adoption,
diagnostic and repair receipts. Preserve unsuccessful results and compare like with like.
Tune model/API/reasoning, objective, sampling effort or remaining allocation deliberately.
Use requested objectives/current presets, not historical model/weight defaults. Keep judge
routing pinned when changing classifiers.

Use ordinary native evaluation when testing changed models or detector definitions.
Loading and publishing preserve saved scores. More workers primarily affect throughput. A repair that fixes a motivating case but loses a retained positive should be
held/rejected, not silently adopted. Do not repeat unchanged comparisons to get a better grade,
or reduce quality/support thresholds to meet a requested detector count.

## Inspection, repairs and external candidates

Inspect actual task/trace/image artifacts when summaries are insufficient. Record exact
source-qualified IDs, artifact/claim hashes, evidence and uncertainty. Wrong answers or
plausible stories alone do not prove a causal mechanism. Preserve ambiguous benchmarks,
contrary cases and denominators; do not exclude them silently.

Run candidate variants outside the live library when appropriate. Before contribution:

1. Coordinate native ownership/lock and checkpoint. Preserve original definitions, helpers,
   dependencies, controls, reviews and exposure history.
2. Preview source-qualified mappings and same-name collisions; resolve replacements deliberately.
3. Import an **unscored candidate** through native transfer/registration. Never transplant
   source-run grades, predictions, match sets or final-validation receipts.
4. Record origin library/version/author/source hash and driver edits/rationale. Register every
   external inspection/control through native exposure/reservation machinery. Previously seen
   examples cannot become fresh confirmation through transfer or Continue.
5. Have AutoFA admit, evaluate, optimize and review normally. Attribute subsequent native work
   separately from external authorship. Resolve missing exposure integration before claiming
   independent evidence; disclose any remaining limitation.

Transfers can go between individual and combined-source analyses in either direction. Preserve
source membership, fields and lineage. Favor coherent executable mechanisms over catch-all
answer-correctness tests or benchmark-specific exceptions designed only to inflate recall.

For an active owner, use the discovered native contribution request queue with stable ID,
expected revision and source/library preconditions. Verify its applied/rejected/deferred receipt;
do not externally replace a live library. Have AutoFA carry out ordinary admission, evaluation,
review and optimization. An acknowledged import is not a GOOD detector or a measured gain.
Subsequent native improvements and driver authorship are separate claims.

## Defects and native follow-ups

Separate classifier limitations from image/transport/tool defects. Refusal is an unresolved
typed abstention, not negative evidence. Respect explicit output limits; bounded recovery of
genuine implicit truncation or malformed labels must retain successful work and expose failures.
Image optimizations must preserve decoded pixels and verified harness delivery. Smaller bytes
or a staged experiment do not prove better semantics; sanitize provider error diagnostics.

Follow [operations.md](operations.md) for authorized runtime fixes. Prefer recurring capabilities
in AutoFA to permanent driver patches or supplied detector answers. Record why native discovery
did not synthesize a family, compare a permitted profile, investigate uncovered evidence or
repair a contradiction. Evaluate native changes on controlled unassisted runs with equal starting
data/budget and separate held-out audits. Never call an assisted campaign an autonomy test.

Keep the lessons from previous interventions explicit: an unreproduced execution defect does
not justify bypassing native repair admission; a retained positive regression can reject a
higher-recall variant; fixing formatting or image delivery is not proof of accuracy improvement.
A configured worker count can hide a lower effective cap or queue bottleneck. Measure throughput
before claiming speedup, reallocate within the campaign's aggregate capacity and back off on
connection/rate errors. A client upload-limit increase cannot solve a server rejection. Never
place discovery coaching in shared judge skill snapshots; correct such leakage openly rather
than preserving a favorable score under changed review conditions.
